# SEO plan for vasay.ai: summary

Smart Solutions, Powered by VASAY.AI · 24 November 2026 · Built with the agency toolkit workflow (steps 0–12)

## The three things that matter most

1. **Google cannot see the site yet.** vasay.ai shows GoDaddy's "One moment, please..." security page to search engines, so nothing can rank until this is switched off for bots and the latest build is deployed on GoDaddy. This comes before everything else.
2. **Directories own the "service + city" searches.** Clutch appeared in all 12 searches checked; DesignRush and Semrush Agency Partners in many. Getting listed and reviewed there is the fastest route to leads.
3. **AI automation / GoHighLevel is an open field in Canada.** Results are gigs and courses, with almost no Canadian agency pages. vasay.ai already has a strong page for it; improving it and adding two articles is the best early organic win.

## Where the site stands

**Strong:** titles, descriptions, headings, structured data, answer-first content, speed on desktop, clean technical setup (all pages 200, in the sitemap, one H1, alt text, consistent name, address and phone).

**Behind competitors:**
- No case study pages.
- Service pages are shorter and carry little proof.
- One blog article.
- No reviews or directory profiles.
- No Google Business Profile confirmed.

## The plan

- **December:** fix indexing on GoDaddy, Search Console and GA4, Business Profile, directory listings, reviews, `/work` with the two serik.ca case studies, the "SEO cost in Toronto" article.
- **January:** AI automation page upgrade, GoHighLevel and missed-call articles, first monthly report.
- **February:** three development case studies, website cost and Google Ads budget articles, real testimonials.
- **Pace:** 2 new pieces a month.
- **No city pages yet:** results are the same for every GTA city. City names go into service pages, footer and the Business Profile service area.

## Limits of this research

- The search tool used is US-based. Google's Canadian map pack, People Also Ask and AI answers were not captured.
- No search volumes: there was no Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush export, so priorities rest on what ranks.
- Five competitor sites blocked automated reading.
- PageSpeed could not be re-run today.

A SERP API key or a Keyword Planner export would firm up the numbers.
