## What the captures show

1. **Directories own "service + city" searches.** Clutch appears in all 12; DesignRush, Semrush Agency Partners, TechBehemoths, HelloDarwin and Gartner/DMBX in several. A new agency site will not outrank them soon. **Action: get listed on them** (Clutch, DesignRush, Semrush Agency Partners, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, HelloDarwin). This is the fastest route into these results.
2. **Agency city pages that do rank:** Search Engine People (`/seo-vaughan`), Thrive (`/vaughan-ca-seo-company/`), Ignite Digital (`/canada-seo/ontario/vaughan-search-engine-optimization`). They use one page per service per city. Toolkit rule: only with real local substance, never templated (doorway-page risk).
3. **Price and question searches go to blog articles** ("how much does SEO cost in Toronto"). vasay.ai has no price articles yet: a clear content gap.
4. **AI automation / GoHighLevel is wide open in Canada:** results are gigs, courses and news, with almost no Canadian agency service pages. Best early-win topic.
5. **Cybersecurity search is IT-company territory** (managed IT providers). Position it as "website security", not general cybersecurity.
