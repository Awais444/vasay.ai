## Table stakes (most competitors have them)

1. One page per service, with the city in title and H1.
2. FAQ block with FAQPage schema.
3. Proof: client logos or names, results numbers, case studies.
4. Third-party badges: Clutch, UpCity, Google reviews.
5. Free audit or free proposal as the main call to action.
6. Process section ("how we work").

## Gaps: what vasay.ai can do that few do

1. **AI automation / GoHighLevel for Canadian SMBs**: no Canadian agency page in the captures.
2. **Answer-first content** ("What is local SEO?", cost, timeline) is already on vasay.ai and rarer on competitor pages, which lead with sales copy.
3. **Schema depth**: vasay.ai's Service + ProfessionalService + FAQ graph is fuller than any page checked.
4. **Price and comparison articles** for Toronto (SEO cost, web design cost, Google Ads budget): only small blogs rank for them.

## Patterns that best explain who ranks (directional)

1. Directories rank first; agencies that rank also appear in those directories.
2. Ranking agency pages carry the city in title, H1 and URL.
3. Long, proof-heavy service pages with FAQ.
4. Large content libraries (SEP) or strong service hubs (Konstruct).
5. Awards and review badges on the page.
